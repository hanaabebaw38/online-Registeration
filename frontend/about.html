Our University

About Debre Markos University

Debre Markos University is a public higher education institution serving the Amhara region and Ethiopia through teaching, research, and community engagement. The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate study across fields including science, technology, agriculture, health, business, and the humanities. Its work aims to prepare knowledgeable graduates and contribute to social and economic development.

Mission

Our Mission

  • Prepare knowledgeable, skilled, and attitudinally mature graduates in relevant disciplines to support peace, democracy, and national development, and to make the country internationally competitive.
  • Promote and enhance research focused on knowledge and technology transfer, consistent with the country's priority needs.
  • Design and provide community and consultancy services that cater to the developmental needs of the country through active engagement with the community and stakeholders.

Vision

Our Vision

DMU aspires to be one of the top ten universities in East Africa by 2030.

University Achievements

Success Stories of DMU

National accreditation for CJEBS

In August 2026, the Choke Journal of Education and Behavioral Studies earned national research journal accreditation through an evaluation conducted by Ethiopia's Ministry of Education and the Ethiopian Research Editors' Association. DMU reported that the recognition is valid for three years, strengthening the journal's role as a platform for education and behavioral-science research.

Read DMU's accreditation announcement

13th National Research Conference

DMU hosted its 13th National Research Conference on April 24-25, 2026, under the theme "Applied and Innovative Research for Sustainable Development." The two-day hybrid conference featured 33 research papers and brought together researchers from DMU and other universities to discuss how research can address community needs.

Read DMU's conference report

Partnership for doctoral study in atmospheric science

In 2026, DMU, the Space Science and Geospatial Institute, and the Ethiopian Meteorology Institute established a partnership framework for a PhD program in Applied Atmospheric Science. The program covers climate and atmospheric physics, applied renewable energy, and applied meteorology, supporting advanced research and specialist training in areas important to Ethiopia.
