Campus & Learning

Debre Markos University Campuses

The university's campuses serve students through different academic disciplines and learning communities. Explore the role, location, and academic areas of each campus below.

Debre Markos

Main Campus

Located in Debre Markos, Amhara Region, the Main Campus is the university's central and largest campus. It brings together a broad range of academic colleges and institutes, serving as a primary center for teaching, research, and university services.

Colleges and schools include Natural and Computational Sciences, Social Sciences and Humanities, Business and Economics, Agriculture and Natural Resources, and the School of Law. The campus also hosts the Institute of Technology and the Institute of Land Administration.

Burie

Burie Campus

Located in the nearby town of Burie, this multidisciplinary campus extends the university's teaching and community engagement beyond Debre Markos. Its programs span agriculture, natural resources, business, computing, and social development.

Areas of study include Plant Science, Horticulture, Animal Science, Veterinary Science, Natural Resource Management, General Forestry, Agribusiness and Value Chain Management, Computer Science, Accounting and Finance, Economics, Management, and Peace and Development.

Health Sciences

Health Campus

The Health Campus brings together DMU's health-related education and professional training programs. Its schools and departments cover clinical care, public health, laboratory science, medicines, and health information.

Areas of study include the School of Medicine, Public Health, Human Nutrition, Nursing including Pediatrics, Midwifery, Medical Laboratory Sciences, Pharmacy, Health Informatics, Environmental Health, and Pathology.

Coordinating Center

Bichena Coordinating Center

DMU's official project reporting mentions infrastructure work for a Bichena Distance Education Center Library. University reporting has also described Bichena Hospital as a practical training site for Health Science students and documented community-service activities in Bichena, including heritage-preservation training.

Together, these reports connect DMU's work in Bichena with distance learning, practical health education, and community engagement. The reports refer to projects and activities from different dates; contact DMU for current services and program availability.

Coordinating Center

M/Marian Coordinating Center

DMU's official Community Service information identifies Mertu le Mariam as a traditional monastery in the university's surrounding area and describes local monasteries as important cultural-heritage resources and potential settings for research and community-service work.

DMU's campus listing separately names the M/Marian Coordinating Center, but the available official pages do not provide center-specific program or service details. Contact the university to confirm current information about the center.
