Our University

About Debre Markos University

Debre Markos University is a public higher education institution serving the Amhara region and Ethiopia through teaching, research, and community engagement. The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate study across fields including science, technology, agriculture, health, business, and the humanities. Its work aims to prepare knowledgeable graduates and contribute to social and economic development.

Campus & Learning

Our Campuses

Discover the university's main campus and learning areas in business and health.

Main Campus

Located in the city of Debre Markos, Amhara Region, this is the central hub and the largest campus of the university.

Colleges and institutes include Natural and Computational Sciences, Social Sciences and Humanities, Business and Economics, Agriculture and Natural Resources, the Institute of Technology, the Institute of Land Administration, and the School of Law.

Burie Campus

Located in the nearby town of Burie, this campus serves as an independent, multidisciplinary academic center that extends the university's reach.

Programs include Plant Science, Horticulture, Animal Science, Veterinary Science, Natural Resource Management, General Forestry, Agribusiness and Value Chain Management, Computer Science, Accounting and Finance, Economics, Management, and Peace and Development.

Health Campus

Health study areas include the School of Medicine, Public Health, Human Nutrition, Nursing including Pediatrics, Midwifery, Medical Laboratory Sciences, Pharmacy, Health Informatics, Environmental Health, and Pathology.

Unique Initiatives

Explore the region's natural resources, agricultural potential, and rich heritage through learning and community-focused initiatives.

Choke Watershed

Choke Mountain is a known bio-diversity rich hotspot area found South of Lake Tana in Amhara Region, east Gojam Zone. It is a water tower of the upper Blue Nile Basin and a source of over 60 rivers and 270 springs. Choke watershed extend from tropical alpine environments at over 4000m elevation to the hot and dry Blue Nile gorge that includes areas below 1000m elevation, and contain a diversity of slope forms and soil types.

Partners and Collaboration

DMU's published reports document national and international collaboration in teaching, research, technology transfer, and community development.

Universities

National and international academic partners

DMU reports collaboration with Bahir Dar University on Edu4Geo, alongside international partners BOKU University and TU Wien, to develop Geomatics education. The Li4LaM consortium also includes the University of Ljubljana, the University of Agriculture in Krakow, Wollo University, and Addis Ababa University, working on land-management curricula and academic capacity.

Research Institutions

Joint research, data, and doctoral training

The Space Science and Geospatial Institute and Ethiopian Meteorology Institute partnered with DMU on an Applied Atmospheric Science PhD framework. DMU also reports agreements with the Ethiopian Biodiversity Institute and Ethio-Geospatial Information Institute for work in biodiversity, natural resources, geospatial data, applied research, and training.

Organizations

Community and development partnerships

DMU reports a community-development project with Agri-Service Ethiopia and Bread for the World focused on food security, livelihoods, climate adaptation, and natural-resource management in Shebel Berenta. It also reports work with the East Gojjam Zone Justice Department, supported by UNODC, to expand access to legal aid for vulnerable communities.
